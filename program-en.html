Program

Together at sea. Together we discover. Beyond the ordinary.

"Travel helps us understand the beauty of space and the pricelessness of time."

— Georgy Aleksandrov

At Arruno Exploration, everything is driven by one idea: sailing beyond the ordinary. Aboard the sailing vessel Saftingæ, we gather those open to adventure, inspiration, and personal growth. You won't just be a passenger, but part of a crew where trust is valued and mutual support is supported.

Our journeys aren't just sea voyages. They're inner journeys where you'll experience the strength of a crew, expand your own boundaries, and understand how the sea simultaneously makes you both small and great. We create a space for genuine encounters, self-discovery, and unique moments that stay with you—far from the hustle and bustle of routine.

We believe that the right people meet at the right time to share extraordinary experiences. This is what makes every Safting voyage truly unique.

Let yourself be inspired by our itineraries, choose a trip that speaks to you, and start your Out of the Ordinary experience right here – explore our program.
